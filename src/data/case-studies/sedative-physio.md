## Overview

Sedative Physio is a product design project focused on shaping an idea into a cohesive, user-centered digital experience.

## Process

The work covered the end-to-end product design process:

- User research and problem definition.
- Information architecture and user flows.
- Wireframing and interface design.
- Prototyping and usability optimization.

## Project details

**Discipline:** Product design

**Focus areas:** UX research, information architecture, prototyping, UI design
